Here is a complete, production-ready prototype for the LinePulse Control Hub. This interactive single-page application implements the entire specification: - Configurable Early-Warning Playbooks (thresholds, step-by-step response procedures, and escalation rules). - Unified Dashboard View (cross-line visibility, color-coded health states, risk counters, filters). - Dual Signal Ingestion Engine (live automated IoT simulator + manual operator log entry form). - Responsibility Assignment (primary/backup engineers, reassignment audit trail). - Escalation Workflow Management (Open → Acknowledged → In Progress → Resolved / Escalated, action logs, quick auto-escalation simulator). - Last Repair Information (prominently surfaced context, repair history, and auto-logging upon alert resolution). - Simulated Notification Dispatcher (push/email feeds for critical warnings and supervisor escalations). LinePulse Control Hub - Early-Warning Machine Health
What is Included in this Prototype: 1. Dual Signal Ingestion Engine - Automated IoT Stream: Runs every 5 seconds, jittering telemetry on machines and feeding values directly to the rules engine. - Manual Operator Log: Click + Manual Log in the header to submit field gauge readings (temperature, vibration, runtime) and technician observation notes. 2. Configurable Early-Warning Playbooks - Click the Health Playbooks tab to view or edit playbook parameters: - Warning & Critical thresholds for Temperature, Vibration RMS, and Runtime. - Specific step-by-step checklist procedures for warning vs. critical states. - Escalation SLA timeouts and designated escalation supervisor roles. 3. Unified Dashboard View - Live KPI counters for Critical Risks, Early Warnings, Escalated Alerts, and Monitored Equipment. - Interactive Filtering: Filter by Line (Stamping, CNC, Packaging), Health State, or click "Only My Assigned Alerts". - Live Risk Stream: Color-coded cards displaying the breached sensor value vs. playbook limit, current owner, and status. 4. Last Repair Information (PRD Feature #7) - Displayed directly on every machine card and in the deep-dive modal. - Gives technicians immediate historical context (date of repair, actions taken, parts replaced) while troubleshooting. - When resolving an alert, the system automatically prompts the user to record the repair summary and parts used, which updates the machine's permanent ledger. 5. Responsibility Assignment & Escalation Workflow - Every alert is automatically bound to the machine's primary/backup owner. - Supports live status transitions: Open \rightarrow Acknowledged \rightarrow In Progress \rightarrow Resolved. - Auto-Escalation Engine: Background timer triggers an escalation if an alert remains unacknowledged beyond the playbook SLA, updating status to Escalated and dispatching high-priority alerts to the Plant Supervisor. - Click Reassign Owner on any alert to reassign responsibility with an auditable reason. 6. Notification Dispatcher Simulation - Click the notification bell to view real-time push and email dispatches triggered by rule breaches, escalations, or manual entries.
